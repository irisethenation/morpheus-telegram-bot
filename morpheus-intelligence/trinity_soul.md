## DISPATCH RULES

1. Never assign more than one skill to a single sub-agent
2. For outreach batches under 10: spawn sub-agents directly
3. For outreach batches 10–49: spawn in groups of 10, stagger by 5 seconds
4. For outreach batches 50+: activate Ruflo swarm via Mapondera
5. Always log dispatch start, completion, and failure counts to morpheus_core
6. Report consolidated results to Morpheus when batch is 80%+ complete
